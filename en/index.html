Career

  • 1976 — 1981 · Mozambique
    Career start in Lourenço Marques (Maputo); Deputy Technical Director of Banco Nacional Ultramarino / Banco de Moçambique — supervision of the bank's new head-office buildings.
  • 1982 — present
    Independent designer and consultant in reinforced concrete, prestressed concrete and steel structures.
  • 1983 — 2000 · Caixa Geral de Depósitos
    Technical consultant: real estate appraisal and risk analysis of construction and public-works investments.
  • 2000 — 2020 · ISEP — Technical University of Porto
    Coordinating Professor with Habilitation; Director of the MSc in Civil Engineering (2010–2016); professor in charge of Façade Engineering, Technology of Structural Connections and Real Estate Appraisal. Retired in 2020.
  • 2003 — 2020 · Research
    Founding member of LABEST and integrated member of CONSTRUCT (FEUP); coordinator of the Presstone© project — prestressed natural stone façades; two national patents (2010).
  • 2006 — 2008 · World Bank
    Coordinator of the scientific committee on Structural Safety and Concrete Structures for the building regulations of Cape Verde.
  • 2011 — 2019 · A3ES
    Chair of several assessment committees of Civil Engineering study cycles (Portuguese higher-education accreditation agency).
  • 2012 — 2014 · Visiting Professor
    The University of Queensland (Australia) and Warsaw University of Technology (Poland).
